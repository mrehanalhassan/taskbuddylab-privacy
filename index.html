Privacy Policy

TaskBuddyLab for Chrome · version 1.1.0 · effective 31 October 2026

In short: TaskBuddyLab keeps your tasks and settings in your browser's extension storage on your device, plus a private copy in your own Google account through Chrome Sync so they appear on all your Chrome devices (you can turn this off). MR ToolLab does not run a server for TaskBuddyLab and does not receive, sell or share your data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Apart from Chrome Sync, the only time anything leaves your device because of TaskBuddyLab is when you use the search box (see section 4).

1. What TaskBuddyLab stores, and where

Everything below is saved with Chrome's chrome.storage.local API, on your computer, inside your Chrome profile.

DataWhy
Tasks: title, notes (rich text), deadline, priority, category, tags, colour, pin, assigned character, reminder choices, snooze, created/updated/completed timesThe core task manager.
Settings: design, wallpaper, theme colour, character, reminder, quiet-hours, motion and widget preferences, shortcuts, and the optional name you enter for greetingsRemembering your choices.
Layouts from Customize, including any background image you uploadRestoring your customized workspace. Uploaded images are resized and stored locally.
Activity history (last 400 events such as "created", "completed", "rescheduled", with task title and time)The History view in the task manager.
Reminder bookkeeping (which reminders already fired) and dismissed deadline signsMaking sure each reminder is shown only once.
Automatic backups: copies of your tasks, settings and layouts (daily, before every update, and before imports, restores and "Delete all data")So your data can be recovered if anything goes wrong. Restorable in Settings → Sync & backup.
Sync bookkeeping: IDs and times of deleted tasks, and when settings last changedSo edits and deletions reach your other devices correctly.

Some small per-page preferences may also be kept by the browser for the extension's own pages. TaskBuddyLab does not use cookies.

2. Chrome Sync

Your tasks, settings and layouts are also copied with Chrome's chrome.storage.sync API. If you are signed in to Chrome and Chrome sync (including Extensions) is on, Google stores this copy as part of your Chrome sync data so that:

  • the same tasks and settings appear on every Chrome signed in to your Google account, and changes on one device reach the others;
  • your data comes back if you remove your Chrome profile or move to a new computer.

This copy lives in your own Google account under Google's Privacy Policy. MR ToolLab cannot access it. Activity history, reminder bookkeeping, automatic backups and images you upload are not synced. You can turn sync off in TaskBuddyLab → Settings → Sync & backup, or in Chrome's own sync settings.

4. Network requests

  • No remote code. All scripts, fonts, images and character art ship inside the extension. TaskBuddyLab works offline.
  • Web search box. When you submit a search, your browser navigates to Google Search (or to the address you typed), exactly as if you had typed it in the address bar. Google's privacy policy applies to that search.
  • Live search suggestions. The text you type into TaskBuddyLab's search box is sent to Google's suggestion service (suggestqueries.google.com) to show suggestions, without cookies. Google's privacy policy applies. If permission is not granted, suggestions are skipped and search still works. TaskBuddyLab never saves your searches.
  • Chrome Sync is handled by Chrome itself, between your browser and your Google account (section 2).
  • TaskBuddyLab never reads the web pages you visit and never collects your browsing history.

5. Characters and "smart" behaviour

Character moods, dialogue and reminders are produced by local rules and a built-in list of lines. TaskBuddyLab does not use any AI model or external language service, and your tasks are never sent anywhere to generate text.

6. Your control

  • Export a JSON backup any time (Settings → Sync & backup or Privacy & data). The file is saved to your computer only.
  • Restore any automatic backup from Settings → Sync & backup.
  • Turn off Chrome Sync for TaskBuddyLab in Settings → Sync & backup.
  • Delete all TaskBuddyLab data from Settings → Privacy & data → Delete all data. With sync on, this also deletes it on your other synced devices. Automatic backups can be deleted separately in Settings → Sync & backup.
  • Uninstalling TaskBuddyLab removes its local data from Chrome. The Chrome Sync copy stays in your Google account (so reinstalling brings it back) until you delete your data in TaskBuddyLab or clear your Chrome sync data in your Google Account.
  • Optional permissions can be removed at chrome://extensions → TaskBuddyLab → Details.

7. Children

TaskBuddyLab does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. MR ToolLab does not collect any data at all.
